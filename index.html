About

I am a software engineer. These are the projects I keep after hours — mostly desktop applications that talk to hardware, and mobile applications that keep track of things I would otherwise lose.

The work tends to start with a machine on the bench or a job I got tired of doing by hand, which keeps it honest: a laser either fires on the right line or it does not, a stencil either lifts off the steel in one piece or it does not. I write the vocabulary down before the code, keep the model separate from whatever is drawing it, and lean on tests for the parts where being nearly right is worse than being obviously wrong.
